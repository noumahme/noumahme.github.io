Research

Current and recent research.

PhD and master's research at the Tandy School of Computer Science, University of Tulsa.

LLM inference

KV-cache eviction across architectures

Designed an evaluation framework for key-value cache eviction strategies and found that their rankings did not transfer between LLM architectures, in a paired comparison on Qwen3-8B and Llama-3.1-8B.

  • KV-cache compression
  • Qwen3-8B
  • Llama-3.1-8B

LLMs for security

LLM-generated attack graphs

Engineered an LLM-based framework that generated over 100 attack graphs from NVD and CVE vulnerability data, reducing manual analysis time by 40%.

  • LLMs
  • NVD / CVE
  • Attack graphs

NLP and curriculum design

Semantic analysis of the NICE Cybersecurity Framework

Built a Sentence Transformers pipeline with permutation testing over 2,280 NICE statements, showing core competencies form the corpus's semantic center (p < 10-4) and using them to derive learning outcomes for an Introduction to Cybersecurity course.

  • Sentence Transformers
  • Permutation testing

ML for cultural heritage

Pose estimation of West Mexican ceramic figures

Applied MCA and PCA to pose-estimation data from a museum collection of ancient ceramic figures and organized them into posture clusters. Published at EVA London 2026; journal extension in submission.

  • MCA / PCA
  • Clustering
  • Pose estimation

Reinforcement learning

Sustaining cooperation in multi-agent systems

Modeled and implemented a reinforcement learning approach to sustain cooperation among agents in multi-agent systems during master's research.

  • Multi-agent RL
  • Cooperation
